## Project context

**PATABA** (Portal Aset Tanah Pemerintah Daerah) is a website-development collaboration with **BPKAD Kota Palu** concerning information management and recording of local-government land assets.

## Role and contributions

I contributed as a **System Analyst** and **UI/UX Designer**. My work included observing existing workflows, analyzing system requirements, preparing scope-of-work documentation, and developing a user-experience concept for a government service.
