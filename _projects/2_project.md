## Project overview

**PATABA** (*Portal Aset Tanah Pemerintah Daerah*) is a website project developed in collaboration with **BPKAD Kota Palu** to support the organization and recording of local-government land asset information.

## My role

I contributed as a **System Analyst** and **UI/UX Designer**, working with the project team to understand the client's needs, discuss priorities, and translate requests into a defined scope and interface direction. This involved observing existing workflows, documenting requirements, and aligning proposed features with the client's expectations.

Through coordination and iterative discussion, the team developed the website to the final refinement stage, with finishing work and deployment remaining.

The project is currently awaiting the next budget cycle. Funding for the current year was unavailable, so finishing and deployment are expected to resume subject to the 2027 fiscal-year budget and further coordination with the client.
