## Community presentations

My presentation and teaching experience has focused on sharing project knowledge with community members in Desa Ambesia Selatan through the Pemberdayaan Desa Binaan (PDB) programme and KKN Tematik. As Research Assistant and student team leader, I worked with the student team to turn project goals into clear materials and practical discussions.

## Ambesia Fish branding and community presentation

I led the student team's branding work for **Ambesia Fish**, a local fishery product identity developed alongside the SITANPAS programme. The work connected visual design with how the community would introduce and present its products.

- **Brand development:** contributed to the logo, packaging direction, and brand guidelines, coordinating design work with fellow students.
- **Presentation and community introduction:** prepared and presented the branding materials to residents, explaining the brand concept and how the identity could be used to support product recognition and promotion.
- **Implementation support:** accompanied the introduction and use of the brand with community participants, and supported the copyright and trademark registration process.

## SITANPAS system socialization

I also supported community-facing sessions introducing the **SITANPAS** fisheries information and marketing platform. The student team explained the digital workflow and IoT-assisted catch-data process in practical terms for local participants, including socialization with four fishing groups.
