## Community presentations

My teaching and presentation experience is currently based on community-facing sessions during the Pemberdayaan Desa Binaan (PDB) programme and KKN Tematik in Desa Ambesia Selatan. I helped translate research and technical work into practical materials for local participants.

### SITANPAS system introduction

I supported the socialization of the SITANPAS digital fisheries information and marketing system, including how the web platform and IoT-assisted catch-data workflow support local fishery activities.

### Ambesia Fish branding

I prepared and presented brand materials for **Ambesia Fish**, introducing the identity and product-presentation concepts to community participants and supporting their implementation.
