I am a final-year undergraduate student in Informatics Engineering at Tadulako University (cohort 2022; expected graduation 2026), working toward completing my thesis and revising a journal manuscript. My academic interests include software engineering, machine learning and data science, human-computer interaction, and IoT-enabled web systems, especially for public-sector and maritime applications.

## Selected projects

**[SITANPAS — Fisheries Catch and Marketing Information System](https://github.com/nara0102/SITANPAS-v2)** is a Pemberdayaan Desa Binaan (PDB) project delivered through KKN Tematik in Desa Ambesia Selatan. As a research assistant to Ir. Dr. Yuli Asmi Rahman, S.T., M.Eng., I served as the student team leader (Ketua Unit) and coordinated field implementation. My contributions span IoT and web integration, community-facing design and adoption, Ambesia Fish branding, and preparation of research outputs.

**PATABA — Regional Government Land Asset Portal** is a website-development collaboration with BPKAD Kota Palu. As System Analyst and UI/UX Designer, I observed workflows, analyzed requirements, prepared scope-of-work documentation, and developed a government-service UX concept. The collaboration is currently paused pending further coordination and budget clarity.
