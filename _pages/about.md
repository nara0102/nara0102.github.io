I am a final-year undergraduate student in Informatics Engineering at Tadulako University (cohort 2022), working toward completing my thesis and revising a journal manuscript. My academic interests include software engineering, machine learning and data science, human-computer interaction, and IoT-enabled web systems, especially for public-sector and maritime applications.

## Selected projects

**[SITANPAS — Fisheries Catch and Marketing Information System](https://github.com/nara0102/SITANPAS-v2)** is a thematic community-service (KKN Tematik) project for South Ambesia. My roles include Research Assistant, Team Leader, and Lead UI/UX and Front-End Developer. My contributions include system architecture, web and mobile interface design for non-technical users, branding for Ambesia Fish, and preparation of a research manuscript.

**PATABA — Regional Government Land Asset Portal** is a website-development collaboration with BPKAD Kota Palu. As System Analyst and UI/UX Designer, I observed workflows, analyzed requirements, prepared scope-of-work documentation, and developed a government-service UX concept. The collaboration is currently paused pending further coordination and budget clarity.
